# Wreckage & Post-Disaster Inspection: Parallax Hardening
**Operational Grade:** S-Grade Tactical / Forensic Recovery
**Focus:** Mitigation of Parallax Chaos and Jagged-Edge Dissociation

## **1. The Wreckage Crisis**
Inspecting collapsed structures, downed aircraft, or maritime wrecks presents a "Jagged Geometry" problem. Unlike clean infrastructure, wreckage is non-linear and unpredictable.
*   **Parallax Chaos**: In a debris field, objects at different depths overlap in ways the brain cannot quickly resolve via a 2D-to-3D voxel stream, causing "spatial stutter."
*   **Depth-Perception Collapse**: Without flat surfaces or known angles, the pilot loses the ability to judge the gap between the drone/robot and sharp debris, leading to equipment snags.

## **2. Sovereign Hardening Solutions**
*   **Edge-Lattice Enforce**: The engine forensically isolates "jagged" transients and stabilizes them against the background. This ensures that a protruding piece of rebar or a jagged hull plate is rendered with deterministic sharp edges, preventing "visual bleed."
*   **Geometric Grounding**: In a debris field with no floor or ceiling, the **Vitrified Horizon Anchor** is replaced with a **Relative Geometric Anchor**, which locks the operator's perspective to the most stable structural mass in the vicinity.
